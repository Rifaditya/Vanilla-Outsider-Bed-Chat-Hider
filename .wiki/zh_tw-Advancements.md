## 📌 Absence Policy & Vanilla Progression

Bed Chat Hider does NOT inject custom advancement JSONs:
* The mod modifies only screen widget presentation and does not intercept player sleep statistics or criteria triggers.
* Earning the vanilla **"Sweet Dreams"** advancement (`minecraft:adventure/sleep_in_bed`) triggers normally the first time you sleep in a bed.
