## 🌟 The Vision of Vanilla Outsider Sleeping

In Vanilla Minecraft, sleeping instantly fast-forwards the world while dimming the screen with a heavy black vignette and cluttering the view with the chat log and command input box.

When combining **Bed Chat Hider** with **True Sleep**:
1. **True Sleep** accelerates time smoothly, simulating real astronomical celestial movement (sun setting, stars rotating, moon tracking across the sky, rain clouds rolling past).
2. **Bed Chat Hider** clears the chat overlay, providing an uninterrupted cinematic window into the nighttime world!
